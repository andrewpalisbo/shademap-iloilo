Important Notice

This application provides an approximate solar-position calculation based on the selected date, time, and location. Building shadows are simulated using available building footprints and estimated building heights where necessary. The displayed shadows are not verified real-world shadows. The simulation does not account for every tree, structure, terrain feature, or obstruction. It does not measure ground temperature or predict actual outdoor temperatures.
